FAQ

Questions, answered simply.

What is The Learn Vista?

TLV is a student-focused platform concept for learning resources, exam information, scholarships, opportunities and event media.

Will TLV have student accounts?

Not in Version 1. The first release is intentionally lightweight and static. Authentication can be added later if it becomes necessary.

Where will event photos and videos go?

They will be separated into Academic Events and General Events, with dedicated spaces for both photos and videos.

How should scholarship information be verified?

Use TLV as an organized discovery layer, then verify eligibility, dates and application instructions at the relevant official source.
